# Version 4

## How this version differs from the others

Version 4 is the most deliberately staged of the numbered versions. It opens with a loading screen, then presents the dark watch campaign and a collection carousel with product-themed titles and descriptions. Compared with v3, it gives the collection a clearer voice and adds a more theatrical opening, while hiding several secondary navigation and settings elements for a pared-back presentation.

- **Compared with the root page:** the two share the product story and descriptive collection cards; v4 adds the opening loading screen, while the root page moves directly into the campaign.
- **Compared with v1:** v4 expands the single watch-focused hero into a longer campaign and collection story.
- **Compared with v2:** v4 replaces the early version's generic carousel labels with product-themed copy and adds a loading introduction.
- **Compared with v3:** v4 adds the loading introduction and descriptive collection cards, while v3 keeps more navigation visible and leaves its carousel labels generic.

## Experience

This is the most polished numbered direction for visitors who prefer a cinematic entrance and a fuller sense of the collection, presented with fewer visible secondary controls.
