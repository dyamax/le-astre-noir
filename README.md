# L'Astre Noir

## About the project

L'Astre Noir is my original watch concept.  
  
I designed the watches from scratch in Blender and created this project to bring my idea for a dramatic, dark-hued luxury collection to life. The watch face is inspired by a night sky: its deep black surface is scattered with tiny white spots that represent stars. This celestial detail gives the timepiece its distinctive identity and ties the design to the name L'Astre Noir (from French: The Black Star).

## How the versions differ

The website explores the concept across four numbered versions. The root page is the current presentation; `versions/v1` through `versions/v4` preserve earlier directions and refinements.

- **Root page:** The most complete presentation of the product story and collection cards, without v4's opening loading screen. Compared with v1, it is a full page rather than a single watch-focused hero; compared with v2 and v3, its collection cards have descriptive copy instead of generic labels.
- **v1:** The simplest and most product-focused direction: a watch, a moody fog backdrop, a short collection message, and familiar shopping-style controls. Unlike the later versions, it does not take visitors through an extended product story or collection showcase.
- **v2:** The first broader, scroll-through experience, bringing together a product introduction, closer-look section, brand story, and collection carousel. It is an exploratory version: the collection cards are generic, and its “get inspired” prompt does not yet guide visitors into the page.
- **v3:** Builds on the extended presentation with a working “get inspired” journey and a more developed flow through the product and brand story. Like v2, its collection carousel still uses generic card labels; unlike v4, it has no opening loading screen and keeps more of the page's navigation and settings visible.
- **v4:** The most curated numbered version: it adds an opening loading screen and replaces generic collection labels with product-themed titles and descriptions. Compared with the root page, its main distinction is that loading introduction; compared with v3, its collection feels more fully written while secondary navigation and settings are tucked away.

The unnumbered [`versions/backup`](./versions/backup/) folder is an archived snapshot, not an additional numbered version.

## Browse the versions

Open a version directly by adding `/versions/<version>/` to the site's URL, replacing `<version>` with `backup`, `v1`, `v2`, `v3`, or `v4`. For example, use `/versions/v1/` to open version 1 or `/versions/backup/` to open the backup.
