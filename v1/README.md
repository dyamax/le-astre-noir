# Version 1

## How this version differs from the others

Version 1 is the most compact and commerce-oriented interpretation. Its center is a single watch presentation framed by atmospheric fog, a short series message, availability labels, and familiar shopping controls. Unlike the root page and versions 2–4, it does not continue into a guided product story, close-look presentation, or collection carousel.

- **Compared with the root page:** v1 is a standalone hero rather than a long-form brand experience. The root page carries visitors through the product story and presents named collection cards.
- **Compared with v2:** v1 keeps the focus on the watch and shopping-style navigation; v2 expands the page into multiple story sections and a carousel.
- **Compared with v3:** v1 has no guided “get inspired” journey or extended product narrative. v3 develops the page into a scroll-through presentation, even though its carousel labels are still generic.
- **Compared with v4:** v1 offers a shorter, more conventional opening, while v4 adds a loading introduction and detailed product-themed collection cards.

## Experience

Expect a focused first impression: the watch, a dark atmospheric backdrop, the “series 0001” message, and a concise luxury tagline. It is the clearest choice for the earliest, simplest visual direction.
